About Me

I have been working as a professional Chinese teacher for over 8 years. Over the years, I have taught numerous learners coming from the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea and other regions across the globe.

I follow a student-centered teaching philosophy and never apply rigid, uniform course materials to everyone. Each learning plan is tailor-made according to your current Chinese proficiency, personal learning goals and available schedule. Whether you want to master daily conversational Chinese, prepare systematically for HSK examinations, or learn professional Chinese for business scenarios, I will design an exclusive learning route that fully fits your needs.

Teaching Advantages

01

Customized Course Design

We provide diversified targeted courses, including daily conversation courses, HSK exam preparation courses, advanced business Chinese courses and specialized courses for heritage learners. All teaching content will be adjusted dynamically based on your learning progress.

02

Immersive & Interactive Classroom

Classes are mainly delivered in Chinese to build a pure language environment. I adopt various forms such as role-play and real-scene practice to make learning vivid and practical. This method helps you move beyond textbook knowledge and effectively improve your real-life listening and speaking skills.

03

Flexible Learning Schedule

All lessons are one-on-one online classes via Tencent Meeting or Zoom. You can arrange class time freely according to your work and study. We support both short-term intensive training and long-term systematic learning.

Teaching Background

With 8 years of professional experience in teaching Chinese as a foreign language, I have worked at the Confucius Institute and several renowned Chinese language institutions in China. My students come from diverse backgrounds including professionals, university students, expatriates and heritage learners.

I specialize in creating customized learning experiences that address each student's unique needs. Rather than following a one-size-fits-all textbook approach, I design every lesson based on your current level, learning style, goals and available time commitment.
