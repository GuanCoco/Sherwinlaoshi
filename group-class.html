Course Introduction

Designed for learners who want to improve daily oral Chinese, social skills and travel communication. Focus on group interaction, role-play and topic discussion. Small class size ensures plenty of speaking practice with a more budget-friendly price compared to 1-on-1 lessons.

Course Content

  1. Daily conversations: living, shopping, dining, transportation and accommodation
  2. Social communication: small talks, hobbies and opinions sharing
  3. Travel expressions: asking directions and sightseeing communication
  4. Interactive practice: role-play, games and group discussion
  5. Pronunciation correction and basic sentence drills. No in-depth grammar tutoring

Class Rules & Booking Policy

  1. Please choose the class according to your Chinese level. Cross-class attendance is not allowed. You may apply for level upgrade after making progress.
  2. Classes run on fixed weekly schedules. Time cannot be changed by individual student after registration.
  3. Standard class lasts 60 minutes. No extra time will be given.
  4. Full group reschedule is allowed if notified 18 hours in advance.
  5. If one student is absent in a 1-to-2 class: the class changes to 1-on-1 and lasts 30 minutes. Both students will have 1 full session deducted.
  6. If a student is absent in a 1-to-3 or 1-to-4 class: the class proceeds as normal at full duration. No refund or make-up for individual absence.
  7. Coupon codes are not valid for group classes. All group courses follow the listed price.
  8. All classes are held online via Tencent Meeting or Zoom. Class link will be sent before class.
  9. Payment: Users in Mainland China via WeChat/Alipay. Overseas users via Alibaba.com Pay (USD).

授课内容

  2. 社交交流：闲聊、兴趣爱好、观点分享
