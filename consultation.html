Pay What You Want

If you are new to learning Chinese and not sure which course fits your needs best, you are welcome to book a 30-minute one-on-one consultation session. This session uses a pay-what-you-want model — after the session, you decide the amount based on your experience.

What You Will Get

  • A thorough assessment of your current Chinese proficiency level
  • Discussion about your learning goals and available study time
  • A customized learning plan tailored to your specific needs
  • Course recommendations that match your objectives
  • Answers to any questions about the teaching approach and curriculum

How It Works

Step 1: Contact me via email to schedule your consultation session.

Step 2: Join the 30-minute online session via Tencent Meeting or Zoom.

Step 3: After the session, pay any amount you feel is fair based on your experience.

Step 4: If you decide to continue, we will arrange your regular course schedule.
